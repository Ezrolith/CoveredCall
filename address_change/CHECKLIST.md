# Moving home — the complete address-change checklist

Everything that needs your new address when you move, grouped by category. Timing key: do items in *(As soon as date is known)* first, then *(1–2 weeks before)*, *(Moving week)*, and *(After the move)*.

> Generated from [`checklist.py`](checklist.py) by [`build.py`](build.py). For the interactive version run `python address_change/app.py`, or just open [`standalone.html`](standalone.html) in a browser.

## Mail & post

- [ ] **[Postal redirection (Royal Mail)](https://www.royalmail.com/personal/receiving-mail/redirection)** *(As soon as date is known)* — Set up at least 3 weeks before the move. Catches everything you forget on this list — do it first.

## Government & official

- [ ] **[Council tax — close account at old address](https://www.gov.uk/find-local-council)** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Tell the old council your move-out date for a final bill.
- [ ] **[TV Licence](https://www.tvlicensing.co.uk/check-if-you-need-one/moving-home)** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Move your licence to the new address — it doesn't follow you automatically.
- [ ] **[Electoral roll / voter registration](https://www.gov.uk/register-to-vote)** *(After the move)* — Re-register at the new address. Also helps your credit score.
- [ ] **[Driving licence (DVLA)](https://www.gov.uk/change-address-driving-licence)** *(After the move)* — Free to update and legally required. Takes minutes online.
- [ ] **[Vehicle log book V5C (DVLA)](https://www.gov.uk/change-address-log-book)** *(After the move)* — Update the V5C for every vehicle you keep. Free online.
- [ ] **[HMRC (income tax, self assessment)](https://www.gov.uk/tell-hmrc-change-of-address)** *(After the move)* — One update covers income tax, NI and child benefit records.
- [ ] **[Council tax — register at new address](https://www.gov.uk/find-local-council)** *(After the move)* — Register with the new council; ask about discounts (single occupancy etc.).
- [ ] **[DWP — benefits / state pension](https://www.gov.uk/tell-dwp-change-of-circumstances)** *(After the move)* — Universal Credit, state pension, child benefit and any other benefits each need updating.
- [ ] **Parking permits** *(After the move)* — Cancel the old resident permit and apply for one in the new area if needed.

## Banking & finance

- [ ] **Mortgage lender / landlord & letting agent** *(As soon as date is known)* — Give contractual notice if renting; tell your lender your correspondence address if you keep the property.
- [ ] **Current / checking accounts** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Update each bank via its app or online banking — usually under Profile → Personal details.
- [ ] **Savings accounts & ISAs** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Easy to forget dormant accounts — statements at an old address are a fraud risk.
- [ ] **Credit cards** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Update every issuer, including store cards you rarely use.
- [ ] **Loans & car finance** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Personal loans, car finance (PCP/HP) and buy-now-pay-later accounts.
- [ ] **Pension providers** *(After the move)* — Workplace and private pensions — lost-pension tracing is a pain later, update now.
- [ ] **Investment / brokerage accounts** *(After the move)* — Brokers, robo-advisers, share registrars and crypto exchanges.
- [ ] **[NS&I / Premium Bonds](https://www.nsandi.com/)** *(After the move)* — Prize warrants go to the registered address.
- [ ] **PayPal & payment apps** *(After the move)* — PayPal, Wise, Revolut etc. — billing address affects card verification.

## Insurance

- [ ] **Home & contents insurance** *(As soon as date is known)* — Must reflect the insured address or cover is void. Arrange cover for the new home from exchange/completion day.
- [ ] **Car insurance** *(As soon as date is known)* — Legally required to update — the premium is recalculated for the new postcode.
- [ ] **Life insurance & income protection** *(After the move)* — Correspondence address only — quick call or online form.
- [ ] **Private health / dental insurance** *(After the move)* — May also change your hospital list and premium.
- [ ] **Pet insurance** *(After the move)* — Premiums vary by postcode; also update the microchip database.
- [ ] **Breakdown cover** *(After the move)* — AA / RAC / Green Flag — home-start cover uses your address.

## Utilities & home services

- [ ] **Broadband / internet** *(As soon as date is known)* — Book the move or new installation early — lead times run 2–4 weeks.
- [ ] **Landline / TV package** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Often bundled with broadband — move or cancel together.
- [ ] **Cleaner / gardener / window cleaner** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Cancel or transfer regular household services.
- [ ] **Electricity & gas** *(Moving week)* — Take final meter readings (photograph them) on move-out day; open an account at the new address and read those meters too.
- [ ] **Water & sewerage** *(Moving week)* — Close the old account with a final reading; register at the new address.
- [ ] **Mobile phone provider** *(After the move)* — Billing address update; check signal at the new address before renewing.

## Health

- [ ] **[GP / doctor](https://www.nhs.uk/service-search/find-a-gp)** *(After the move)* — Register with a practice near the new address; repeat prescriptions follow your registration.
- [ ] **Dentist** *(After the move)* — Find a practice taking new patients — NHS lists fill fast.
- [ ] **Optician** *(After the move)* — Transfer your prescription history to a new branch.
- [ ] **Vet & pet microchip database** *(After the move)* — Register with a new vet and update the microchip record — it's the address on the chip that gets pets home.
- [ ] **Pharmacy / repeat prescriptions** *(After the move)* — Re-point your nominated pharmacy for electronic prescriptions.

## Work & education

- [ ] **Schools / nurseries** *(As soon as date is known)* — If changing catchment areas, contact the new council's admissions team as early as possible.
- [ ] **Employer HR / payroll** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Payslips, P60s and pension correspondence all use this address.
- [ ] **Professional bodies & alumni** *(After the move)* — Memberships, licences to practise, university alumni offices.

## Subscriptions & shopping

- [ ] **Subscription boxes & magazines** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Meal kits, magazines, coffee clubs — anything that ships on a schedule.
- [ ] **Gym membership** *(1–2 weeks before)* — Transfer to a nearer branch or give notice — contracts often need 30 days.
- [ ] **Amazon & online retail default addresses** *(Moving week)* — Change the default delivery address the day you move — mis-delivered parcels rarely come back.
- [ ] **Streaming & app-store billing** *(After the move)* — Netflix/Spotify etc., plus Apple ID and Google account billing addresses.
- [ ] **Loyalty schemes & store accounts** *(After the move)* — Nectar, Boots, supermarket delivery accounts, airline miles.
- [ ] **Food delivery apps** *(After the move)* — Deliveroo/Uber Eats/Just Eat saved addresses — before you hungrily order to the old flat.

## People & other

- [ ] **Old neighbours / new occupants** *(Moving week)* — Leave a forwarding note for anything the redirect misses.
- [ ] **Friends & family** *(After the move)* — Send the new address around — the generated letter below works for this too.
- [ ] **Charities & regular donations** *(After the move)* — Direct-debit charities and membership organisations (National Trust etc.).
